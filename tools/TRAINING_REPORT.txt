Training report — Intent classifier AI Coach 3.1
Algorithm: Multinomial Naive Bayes on binary Vietnamese character 2-3 grams.
Dataset: 120 authored starter examples / 12 labels; train 90, test 30 (stratified, random_state=42).
Held-out accuracy in current local run: 0.633 (19/30); macro F1 approximately 0.60.
This is an initial engineering experiment, NOT a validated user-intent recognizer.
Some labels have just 2-3 evaluation samples; do not claim real-world accuracy.
Safety-critical labels are excluded from ML routing, so they remain rule/Guardian-driven.
Fallback to Gemini if confidence or margin too small.
Recommended next: collect 50-100 independent paraphrases per label from test volunteers with consent,
remove identifiable/private text, separate train/test by writer, cross-validate and recalibrate thresholds.
